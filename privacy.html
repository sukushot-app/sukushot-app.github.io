Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mac app "Sukushot" (the "App"), provided by Kairi Miura (the "Developer", "we", "us"), handles information.

1. Summary

The App does not collect your personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you capture stays on your Mac.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or usage data. The App does not send your data to the Developer's servers, and the Developer does not operate any server for the App.

3. Information processed on your device

To provide its features, the App processes the following only on your Mac. This information is not sent to us or to any third party.

  • Screen images and videos. Screenshots and recordings you make are processed on your Mac. They are saved or copied only when you ask.
  • Screen recording permission. The App asks for macOS "Screen Recording" permission to capture your screen.
  • Camera permission. The App asks for macOS "Camera" permission only to show the screen of an iPhone or iPad that you connect to your Mac. It does not use your Mac's built-in camera.
  • Audio. If you turn the option on in Settings, the App records the sound of apps while you record the screen. It does not use the microphone.
  • Settings. Preferences such as the timer length, keyboard shortcuts, and last-used file format are stored only on your Mac.

4. Purchases and information handled by Apple

Purchases of Pro are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. The App only reads whether you have an active purchase from Apple's system. Apple handles that information under Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party services

The App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social media services (SDKs). If this changes in the future, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

6. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Files you save or copy are yours and remain wherever you put them. Temporary files created while you drag or edit an image or video are removed when the App next starts. To remove the App's settings, delete the App from your Mac.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or process personal data, we have no personal data to access, correct, export, or delete, and we do not sell or share personal information. Requests under laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) therefore do not apply. If you have any question about your privacy, please contact us.

8. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

9. Security

The App runs in the macOS App Sandbox and keeps your captures on your Mac. It does not upload them anywhere.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with its effective date at the top.
