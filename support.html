Support

Sukushot is a menu-bar Mac app for screenshots and screen recording. Add arrows, text and shapes to what you capture, then copy or save it.

For help or bug reports, contact sukushot.support@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

I can't capture or record the screen

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, and turn on Sukushot. macOS may ask you to restart the app.

My iPhone or iPad does not appear

Connect it to your Mac with a cable and tap “Trust” on the device. Turn on Sukushot in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

Where are my captures saved?

Sukushot does not save captures by itself. In the editor, use Copy or Save Image…, or drag the image from the status bar into another app.

How do I change the keyboard shortcuts?

Open Settings → Shortcuts, click the shortcut you want to change, and press the new key combination. A shortcut needs ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥ (or a function key).

What does Pro include?

Screen recording (area, full screen, timed), iPhone/iPad mirroring and recording, custom colors (More Colors…), screenshot translation, and saving as JPEG, HEIC, TIFF or PDF. Screenshots, annotations, the 12-color palette, and copying or saving as PNG are free.

How do I restore purchases?

Use “Restore Purchases” on the purchase screen or in Settings → Pro.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Use “Manage Subscription…” in Settings → Pro, or open the App Store app and go to your account → Subscriptions.

How do I redeem an offer code?

Use “Redeem Code…” on the purchase screen or in Settings → Pro.

Does Sukushot collect my data?

No. Sukushot has no accounts, analytics or ads, and everything you capture stays on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy for details.
