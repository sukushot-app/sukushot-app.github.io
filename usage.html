How to use Sukushot

From the first capture to sharing, in eight short steps. Steps marked Pro need Sukushot Pro.

1

Capture

Click the Sukushot icon in the menu bar and choose Crosshair Screenshot, or press ⌃⌥1. Drag to select the area; crosshair guides through the cursor help you line things up. Timed Crosshair Screenshot (⌃⌥2) waits a few seconds first, and Full Screen Screenshot (⌃⌥3) captures the whole screen. The first time, macOS asks for Screen Recording permission.

2

Mark up

The editor opens right after you capture. Pick a tool on the left (Text, Arrow, shapes, Line) and drag on the image. Change the color with the color button; the 12-color palette is free. Use Crop to keep only the part you need, and Undo and Redo in the top bar.

3

Adjust in place

Click an annotation to select it. A small bar appears right above it. Change the color, width and line style, or the text size and background, or delete it with the red trash button. Changes only affect the selected item.

4

TranslatePro

Press the Translate button in the top bar. Sukushot reads the text in your screenshot and shows the original and the translation side by side, with the translated text listed below so you can copy it. Choose the language, then press Apply to Image to put the translation into the picture.

5

Copy, save and share

Use Copy to put the image on the clipboard, or Save Image… to save it. PNG is free; JPEG, HEIC, TIFF and PDF are Pro. You can also drag the thumbnail in the bottom bar straight into Finder, Messages or any other app.

6

Record the screenPro

Choose Crosshair Recording (⌃⌥4) or Full Screen Recording (⌃⌥6) from the menu; Timed Crosshair Recording is ⌃⌥5. Start, then Stop. App audio is recorded too (you can turn it off in Settings → General). In the editor, drag the yellow handles on the seek bar to trim, then copy, save or drag out the video.

7

Show your iPhone or iPadPro

Connect your iPhone or iPad to the Mac with a cable and tap Trust on the device. Choose Show iPhone/iPad Screen (⌃⌥7), then take screenshots or record the device screen. If asked, turn on Sukushot in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

8

Change the shortcuts

Open Settings → Shortcuts, click a shortcut, and press the new keys (⌘, ⌃ or ⌥ with another key). In General you can also set the timer delay and turn the shutter sound on or off.
